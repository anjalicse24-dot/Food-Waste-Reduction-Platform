How FeedForward Works

01
👤

Register & Choose Role

Sign up as a Donor (restaurant, hostel, home) or Receiver (NGO, volunteer) in under 2 minutes.

02
🍱

Upload Food Details

Donors post food type, quantity, location, expiry time & optional photo. Takes just 30 seconds.

03
📍

NGOs See Nearby Food

Receivers browse available donations sorted by urgency and distance on an interactive map.

04
✅

Claim & Collect

NGO claims the food — status updates from Available → Claimed → Completed automatically.

✦ Features

Everything You Need
to Fight Food Waste

⏳

Expiry Countdown Timer

Live countdown shows urgency — donors know when food must be collected, reducing last-minute waste.

🔥

Priority System

Food expiring soon is automatically boosted to the top, ensuring the most urgent donations are claimed first.

📩

Smart Notifications

Instant alerts when food is available nearby, when a claim is made, or when pickup is confirmed.

📸

Photo Upload

Donors can upload images of food so NGOs can verify quality before claiming. Builds trust.

⭐

Donor Reliability Rating

NGOs rate each donation, building a trust score for donors. Encourages consistency and quality.

📊

Admin Dashboard

Admins monitor total donations, meals saved, active users and waste metrics in real time.
